## 1. Positioning & Messaging

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Primary:** UK micro-creators posting 3–7×/week on TikTok and/or Instagram Reels.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Followers | 500–25k (pre-monetisation or early monetisation) |
| Niche | Beauty, fitness, food, side-hustle, parenting, local services, Etsy sellers |
| Income | £0–£500/mo from content; day job or part-time |
| Behaviour | Films on phone, edits in CapCut, captions take 20–45 min, no content team |
| Mindset | "I know I should post more but I'm stuck on what to say / whether it'll flop" |

**Secondary (Month 3+):** UK small-business owners using Reels for leads (salons, coaches, trades) — especially once the **Conversion Layer** ships.

**Anti-ICP (don't chase):** Agencies, 100k+ creators with editors, YouTube-longform-only creators, US creators who want USD pricing.

---

### Core Pain Points

1. **Caption paralysis** — 30+ minutes per post, still unsure if the hook lands
2. **Posting blind** — no idea if an idea will perform until after filming
3. **Tool sprawl** — ChatGPT + Canva + Later + a caption app = £40+/mo and context-switching
4. **Engagement debt** — comments pile up; replying boosts reach but takes an hour
5. **Views ≠ money** — viral posts with no CTA, no lead magnet, no DM funnel
6. **UK tone gap** — US-trained AI sounds wrong ("y'all", "fall" instead of "autumn")

---

### Value Proposition

**One-liner (live on site):**
> Score it before you film. Post with confidence.

**Supporting line:**
> 6 AI tools, one dashboard, £9.99/mo — captions in seconds, algorithm scores before you hit record, and content plans from a brain dump.

**Supporting proof points:**
- 6 tools, one dashboard, **£9.99/mo** (less than one coffee a week)
- Built for **TikTok + Reels**, not generic "social media"
- **Algo Analyzer** = pre-flight check competitors don't offer at this price
- **Conversion Layer** (roadmap) = the only suite that walks hook → CTA → DM → sale

**Tagline options (pick one):**
- *"Post smarter. Not harder."* (current tone — keep)
- *"From blank screen to posted — in minutes."*
- *"The UK toolkit for creators who actually post."* (differentiation)

---

### Differentiation Matrix

| Competitor | What they do | Clutvi wedge |
|------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Caption.ai** | Captions + basic scheduling | 6-tool workflow; algo scoring; post-history analysis; UK GBP pricing; conversion tools coming |
| **Later** | Scheduling + link-in-bio | Not a scheduler — **creation + strategy** layer; complements Later, doesn't replace it |
| **Metricool** | Analytics dashboards | **Pre-post** scoring, not post-hoc charts; built for creators, not agencies |
| **Opus Clip** | Long → short video clips | Different job — Clutvi owns **words, strategy, monetisation**; pair well in messaging |
| **ChatGPT** | General AI | Purpose-built UI, no prompting; platform-specific outputs; history + gating = workflow |
| **Canva Magic Write** | In-design copy | Standalone creator workflow; algo + planning + replies in one place |

**Positioning statement (internal):**
Clutvi is not "another caption writer." It's the **pre-post intelligence + content ops** layer for UK short-form creators — and the only affordable tool that will connect content to **conversion**.
