FAQ

What makes a good Instagram Reel caption?+

Strong Reel captions open with a hook in the first line, add context, and end with a CTA or question. Hashtags should be niche-specific — 5 to 12 is plenty.

How many hashtags should I use on Reels?+

Most UK creators see best results with 5–12 targeted hashtags. Clutvi generates a balanced mix of niche and discovery tags per caption.
