FAQ

How do I make a TikTok content calendar?+

Brain dump your ideas into Clutvi, pick 5, 10, or 30 days, and get a structured plan with post concepts, formats, and hooks.

How often should I post?+

UK micro-creators typically post 3–7×/week. A 30-day calendar gives you buffer — film batches on weekends, post through the week.

Is this better than a Notion template?+

Templates still need you to fill every cell. Brain Dump generates ideas from what you already know — then you can export to Notion if you like structure.
