## 1. Profile setup (do this before posting anything)

### TikTok — @getclutviapp

**Switch to a Business account.** Settings → Account → Switch to Business Account.

The trade-off, because it's real: Business accounts get a **clickable website link in bio at
any follower count**, but are restricted to TikTok's *Commercial Sounds* library — no trending
viral audio. Creator accounts get the full sound library but no bio link under 1k followers.

For a £9.99/mo SaaS, the bio link wins. Your videos are voiceover-led screen demos, so the
sound library barely matters — you need audio *present*, not audio *trending*. (Verify in-app;
TikTok changes this periodically.)

An empty grid kills profile→link conversion. Instagram visitors judge the grid, TikTok
visitors judge the last video. So **seed 3 posts on IG first**, before post #1 goes on TikTok:

1. Carousel: "6 tools, one dashboard, £9.99" — one slide per tool, screenshots
2. Carousel: "5 hooks that score under 30/100 (and the rewrite)" — pure value, no pitch
3. Reel: Script 7, the founder story

That's an afternoon. Then start the calendar.

## 2. Where the ad video goes

`ad/out/clutvi-ad-9x16.mp4` is a polished 20s product ad. **It is not post #1.**

Cold TikTok reach punishes anything that reads as an advert — no face, no voice, motion
graphics. It will get shown to nobody and start the account off looking dead.

Use it for:

1. **TikTok pinned video, slot 3** — once you have 5–6 organic posts. It's the "what is this
   actually" explainer people watch after a demo hooks them.
2. **Instagram Stories highlight** — cover titled "What is Clutvi", permanent.
3. **Spark Ads later** — the £20–30 paid test in `marketing-plan.md` §10, Day 45+. This is
   what it's genuinely best for.

**It has no audio track content.** Both platforms suppress silent video. Before posting, add
a bed track in-app at 10–15% (Commercial Sounds on TikTok, per §1) or record a 20s voiceover
over it. Don't post it silent.

## 3. Platform split — they are different jobs

Both docs so far treat this as a TikTok exercise. Now that Instagram exists:

| | TikTok @getclutviapp | Instagram @getclutvi |
|---|---|---|
| Job | **Cold discovery** — reach strangers | **Credibility + saves** — convert the curious |
| Wins on | Hook strength, watch time | Saves, shares, carousel depth |
| Format mix | 90% video | 60% Reels, 40% carousels |
| Post first | Script 1 (algo score reveal) | Script 7 (founder story) |
| CTA | "link in bio, 3 days free" | "saved this? the tool's in my bio" |
| Cadence | 5–7/week | 3 Reels + 2 carousels/week |

**Why the different opening post:** Script 1 is a curiosity-gap hook ("31 out of 100") that
works with zero followers because TikTok will show it to strangers on merit. Instagram's cold
reach is worse, so your first IG post is mostly seen by people who already clicked your
profile — and for them the founder story converts harder than a demo.

**Cross-posting rule:** film once, post native to both, but **change the on-screen hook text**
and re-upload the file — don't share TikTok's watermarked export to Reels. Instagram
deprioritises visibly recycled TikToks. Stagger by 24h.

**Instagram-native formats the TikTok kit doesn't cover** — carousels are your saves engine:

- *"5 hooks that score under 30 (and the rewrite that fixed them)"* — 6 slides, real tool output
- *"The £42/mo creator tool stack vs £9.99"* — Script 10 as a carousel
- *"30 days of content from one messy brain dump"* — screenshots of actual Brain Dump output
- *"What your caption is missing"* — 5 slides, teaching, one soft CTA slide
- *"I scored 10 of my own old videos. Here's what the flops had in common."* — best of the lot

Rule for carousels: slides 1–5 teach and give away something real, slide 6 mentions Clutvi
once. Saves are the metric; a hard sell tanks them.

## 6. Automation handoff — week 3, not now

You already own the machinery: the n8n autoposter (`~/.n8n`, container `n8n`) plus an
**upload-post paid plan**, which unlocks TikTok posting *and* server-side scheduling. A Clutvi
workflow could mirror AInofluff's — see the AInofluff setup guide for the pattern and its traps.

**Don't automate weeks 1–2.** The posts that convert for a SaaS this early are founder screen
demos with your voice on them, and no pipeline generates those. Automating now would mean
posting generic AI slop from a zero-follower account.

**What to automate from week 3:**
- Carousel graphics from a planner sheet (Gemini image gen, exactly the AInofluff flow)
- Scheduled re-posts of proven winners to Reels 24h after TikTok
- The Telegram approval step, so nothing goes out unreviewed

**What to keep manual forever:** every screen demo, every talking-head, every reply to a comment.

Known traps if you go this route: a new upload-post *profile* is needed for Clutvi (the existing
ones are `ainofuss` and `naturalhome`); after any sqlite edit you must Save-then-Publish in the
editor or the cron runs the old version; and ngrok is now a systemd user service, so approval
buttons survive reboots.

## 7. Metrics — and when to stop

Check weekly, not daily.

| Metric | Where | Week 4 target |
|---|---|---|
| 3-sec hold rate | TikTok analytics per video | >55% on demo posts |
| Profile visits | Both platforms | 400+/wk combined |
| Bio link clicks | TikTok analytics + IG insights | 60+/wk |
| Saves per carousel | IG insights | 15+ |
| Trial signups attributed | Supabase `profiles` created_at vs post times | 15 |
| Trial → paid | Stripe | 25%+ of those |

**Kill criteria, decided now so you don't rationalise later:**

- A **pillar** with 4+ posts and no post above your median views → drop that pillar
- **Instagram** under 100 profile visits/wk by week 4 → cut to 2 carousels/wk, put the hours
  into TikTok. Don't keep both alive out of fairness.
- **Bio link clicks high but trials near zero** → the problem is the landing page or the
  card-upfront trial, not the content. Stop filming and fix the page.
- 40 posts with under 5 trials → the *positioning* is wrong, not the volume. Rewrite the offer
  before shooting more.

**North star:** trials that generate at least twice in 24h. Everything else is vanity.

## 8. Honest risks

1. **Card upfront on a 3-day trial** is the biggest conversion drag you have, and no amount of
   social will fix it. If clicks are healthy and trials aren't, that's the culprit.
2. **You're the face.** Every high-converting format here needs your voice on it. If that's not
   sustainable, the plan needs rebuilding around faceless formats before week 3, not after.
3. **Dogfooding is the cheapest content you have** — write every caption with Caption Writer and
   screenshot it. "I used my own app to write this" is a post, and it's free.
4. **Nothing has ever been bought on the live Stripe account.** Do the one real end-to-end
   purchase (`~/Notes/Projects/Clutvi.md` item 3) *before* you send strangers to checkout.
   Driving traffic to an unproven payment flow is the one genuinely unrecoverable mistake here.
